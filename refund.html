Refund & Cancellation

Last updated: September 29, 2026 · Applies to the Rescue app

We'd rather be upfront about this than bury it in fine print. Here's exactly how refunds and cancellations work — no jargon.

All payments — monthly and annual plans — are non-refundable.

Cancelling stops future billing, not the current period

You can cancel your subscription at any time. Cancelling stops all future billing — but it does not refund the period you've already paid for. You'll keep access until the end of that period, and you won't be charged again after that.

The free trial is different

Your 14-day free trial can be cancelled anytime before it ends, at no charge. If you cancel before the trial's last day, you will not be billed at all.

If you don't cancel before the trial ends, it automatically converts into a paid subscription (₹99/month or ₹799/year, whichever you selected) and the non-refundable rule above applies from that point on.

How to cancel

  • From the app's Account screen, or
  • From your Play Store subscriptions page, or
  • By emailing sales@fliptle.com and we'll take care of it.

Questions

If something about a charge looks wrong or you're not sure what happened, email us at sales@fliptle.com — we read every message.
