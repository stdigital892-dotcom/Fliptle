Rescue is operated by Fliptle, a sole proprietorship. This page tells you, in plain language, exactly what Rescue actually stores about you, why, and how to get it deleted.

1. What we collect

Grouped by what it's for:

Account

  • Your email address
  • How you signed in — Google, or email and password
  • Your name, if you choose to add one
  • A phone number, if you choose to add one — not used to message you or anyone else today
  • A random install ID the app generates on your device
  • How many times the app has been installed and reinstalled, and when

Protection status

  • Whether your protection is currently on
  • The last time the app checked in
  • Your progress through the uninstall process, if you've started one

Your settings

Saved so they survive a reinstall:

  • Which apps and websites you've chosen to block
  • Your Reels and Shorts switches
  • Your freeze status and timing
  • Your porn-blocking status, start date, and day count

This is sensitive information. Your porn-blocking status and day count say something private about you. We store it only to run the feature you turned on, and we treat it with that in mind.

Payments

  • Your plan and its price
  • Your subscription status
  • Razorpay's payment ID and order ID for each transaction

We never see or store your card number or UPI ID — Razorpay handles that directly.

Website

  • Your email address, if you join the waitlist at fliptle.com
  • Your browser's user-agent text — standard technical information any website receives

Analytics

Firebase (Google) automatically collects an app instance ID, along with basic device and usage statistics — the same as most apps.

2. What we do not collect

We do not collect the pages you visit, what's on your screen, or which other apps you use.

Rescue's Accessibility service checks addresses on your phone against your block list — on your device, as they happen. It does not record or upload them.

3. Why we collect it

  • To run the app and its features
  • To keep your protection and settings working, including through a reinstall
  • To send you emails — a welcome email, or a service update
  • To process your payments
  • To detect uninstalls

4. Who handles it

  • Google (Firebase) — stores your account and app data
  • Razorpay — processes payments
  • Resend — sends our emails

We do not sell your data to third parties — not to anyone, for any reason.

5. Accountability partner

Rescue is building an accountability partner feature. It is not active yet. When it launches: if you choose to add a contact, that person must agree before anything is sent to them. We'll collect only the phone number you provide for them — nothing else about that person.

6. Deleting your data

  • In the app: go to Account → Delete my account
  • Or email sales@fliptle.com

Your data is deleted 72 hours after your request — you can cancel anytime before then. Razorpay may still keep payment records separately, as required by law.

7. Your rights (DPDP Act, 2023)

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct any of your data that is inaccurate or incomplete
  • Delete your account and all associated data, at any time

Rescue is intended for users aged 16 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. For any question, request, or grievance about your data, email sales@fliptle.com — every message is read by a real person.

8. Changes to this policy & contact

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continuing to use Rescue after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Questions about this policy, or a request about your data? Email sales@fliptle.com. See our Contact page for more.
