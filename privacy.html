Rescue is built to help you break free from compulsive porn and social media use. Doing that job well means asking for your trust with your data — so this page tells you, plainly, exactly what we collect, why, and what we deliberately do not touch.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you sign up, we collect your email address — either through Google Sign-In or email/password.

Device identifiers. We collect a device identifier so the app can recognize your device, keep your protection settings active, and detect reinstalls.

Data needed for blocking features to work. To run Rescue's core features, we store:

  • which apps and sites you've chosen (or are required) to block,
  • your freeze status (whether a freeze is active and when it ends),
  • your porn-blocking activation date, used to calculate your streak.

Accountability partner (feature not active yet). Rescue is building an accountability partner feature. When it launches, if you choose to set one up, we will collect only the phone number you provide — nothing else about that person. We won't collect their name, contact list, or any other detail. That number will be used solely to send the status alerts you've explicitly agreed to (for example, a notification if you disable a protection). Until this feature goes live, no accountability-partner data is collected at all.

2. What we do NOT collect

We do not collect or store your browsing history, search content, or the specific content you view.

Rescue's blocking works by checking the URLs you visit against a blocklist stored on your own device. That check happens entirely on-device — the URLs are never logged, uploaded, or transmitted to our servers. We have no record of what sites you visited, what you searched for, or what you looked at.

3. How we use your information

  • To run the app's features — blocking, freeze cycles, streak tracking, and (once live) accountability alerts.
  • To create and manage your subscription.
  • To send you account-related emails — for example, a welcome email or a service update.
  • To respond when you contact us for support.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it to anyone.

4. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on Firebase, Google Cloud's application platform. It is used only to run the app's features and to process your subscription — never for any purpose beyond that.

5. Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay, a licensed Indian payment gateway. We do not store your card number, CVV, or any full payment credentials — Razorpay handles that directly. What we do store, for billing history and support, is limited to your plan, subscription status, and Razorpay's own transaction reference for each payment.

6. Your rights (DPDP Act, 2023)

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct any of your data that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Delete your account and all associated data, at any time.

To delete your account: open the app, go to the Account screen, and use the delete option there — or email sales@fliptle.com and we'll handle it for you.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties, under any circumstances. For any question, request, or grievance about how your data is handled, contact us at sales@fliptle.com — every message is read by a real person.

7. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your associated data, except where a limited retention period is required by law (for example, financial records connected to a payment).

8. Age requirement

Rescue is intended for users aged 16 and older — see our Terms & Conditions. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your data — including encrypted connections and access-restricted infrastructure. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but protecting your data is something we take seriously.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continuing to use Rescue after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request about your data? Email sales@fliptle.com. See our Contact page for more.
