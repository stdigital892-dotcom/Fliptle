Delete your Rescue account

Last updated: 10 October 2026

In the app

  1. Open Rescue and go to Account.
  2. Tap Delete my account and confirm.
  3. Your account and data are deleted 72 hours later. Until then you can tap Cancel deletion in the same place.

By email

Email sales@fliptle.com from the address you signed up with, and we will start the same process.

What is deleted

Your account and sign-in, your settings and progress, your plan selection and subscription record, your protection and install records, your waitlist entry for that email, and — if you added one — your accountability partner's number and note. If your install record still holds a phone number from an older version of the app (Rescue no longer asks for or stores your own phone number), that is deleted with it too.

What is kept

Payment records may be kept as the law requires. Razorpay also keeps its own records. If your accountability partner ever replied STOP, we keep that opt-out record (their number only) so they are never messaged again, even after your account is gone.

Important

  • Payments are not refundable.
  • Deleting your account does not remove Rescue from your phone. Protection that is already on stays active until you uninstall the app from Android settings.
