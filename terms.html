3. The service — how Rescue works

Rescue is a self-control tool designed to help you reduce compulsive adult-content and social media use. We describe its features honestly here, because they're deliberately strict — that strictness is the point.

Adult-content blocking is a one-way switch. Once you enable it, it cannot be turned off from within the app. The only way to remove it is to uninstall the app entirely — which itself requires completing a short math-and-typing challenge every day for 5 days. This is intentional — Rescue is built so your in-the-moment self can't undo what your committed self decided.

Other features include:

  • 3-day freeze system — once started, a freeze locks your commitment to stay off selected distracting apps or sites for its duration; it cannot be shortened or cancelled early.
  • Shorts & Reels control — a limited daily session allowance for short-form video, rather than a total block.
  • New browser auto-block — if you install a new browser app, Rescue automatically applies your blocking rules to it.
  • Exit challenge — removing adult-content blocking, or deleting your account, requires completing 10 short math questions plus a typing exercise, every day for 5 days in a row. Missing a day resets the count.
  • Accountability partner alerts — you may add one accountability partner by mobile number. If you do, and your protection stops reporting before you finish removing the app, we send that partner a WhatsApp message about it. This is entirely opt-in — nothing is sent unless you add a partner and confirm they've agreed.

If you add an accountability partner, you confirm that you have told them and that they are okay receiving these messages before you add their number. Rescue is not responsible for messages sent to a number you entered without that person's consent.

Because these features are designed to resist being disabled in the moment, please make sure you understand them before enabling them.
